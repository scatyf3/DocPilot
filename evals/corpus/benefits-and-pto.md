# Benefits and Paid Time Off

Owner: People Operations. Last updated: January 2, 2025.

This guide summarizes the benefits available to full-time employees of Lumen Labs in the United States. Employees in other countries receive equivalent benefits through a local supplement that People Operations shares during onboarding. Questions go to Grace Mbeki or the #people-ops channel.

## Paid time off (policy LL-HR-07)

The paid time off policy is document LL-HR-07, and this section is its official summary.

- Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid time off per year, credited monthly at roughly 2.08 days per month.
- There is no waiting period: PTO can be used as soon as it has been accrued, including in your first month.
- Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next calendar year. Any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31.
- PTO is requested in BambooHR. Any absence longer than 3 consecutive working days must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance.
- If you are on the on-call rotation, arrange a swap before your PTO starts; your manager will not approve PTO that overlaps an uncovered on-call shift.

## Holidays and sick leave

Lumen Labs observes 10 paid company holidays per year, published each December for the following year. In addition, the whole company is closed from December 24 through January 1. Sick leave is separate from PTO: every employee receives 10 sick days per year, which do not carry over and do not require a doctor's note for absences of up to 3 days.

## Parental leave and sabbatical

All parents, including adoptive and foster parents, receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Employees become eligible after 6 months of employment, and the leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first year after the child arrives.

After 5 years of continuous employment, employees receive a 4-week paid sabbatical. The sabbatical must be taken within 2 years of becoming eligible and does not reduce your PTO balance.

## Health insurance

Medical, dental, and vision insurance are provided through Cedarline Health. Lumen Labs pays 90% of insurance premiums for employees and 75% of premiums for dependents. Coverage starts on your first day of employment. Open enrollment runs during the first two weeks of November each year.

## Retirement

Lumen Labs offers a 401(k) plan administered by Harborstone. The company matches 100% of your contributions up to 4% of your salary, and the match vests immediately.

## Stipends

- Home office: a one-time stipend of $750, which must be spent within your first 60 days.
- Learning and development: $1,500 per calendar year for courses, books, and conference tickets. Claims follow the expense policy.
- Wellness: $50 per month, paid with payroll, for gym memberships, fitness apps, or similar.
- Internet: $40 per month for fully remote employees.

## On-call pay

Engineers on the on-call rotation are paid a stipend for each week they carry the pager. The primary on-call engineer receives $400 per week, and the secondary on-call engineer receives $150 per week. Shifts that include a company holiday pay double for that week. Stipends are paid automatically with the next payroll based on the PagerDuty schedule, so keep the schedule accurate when you swap shifts.

## Payroll

Payroll runs every other Friday through Gusto. If a payday falls on a company holiday, you are paid on the previous business day.
