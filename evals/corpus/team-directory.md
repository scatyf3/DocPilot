# Lumen Labs Team Directory

This directory lists the people you are most likely to need at Lumen Labs, what they are responsible for, and how to reach them. It is maintained by People Operations and was last updated on January 10, 2025. If your role or team changes, ask Grace Mbeki to update this page within one week.

## Company at a glance

Lumen Labs builds Lumen Search, a hosted document-search API used by software companies to add search to their own products. The company was founded in 2021 and had a total headcount of 87 as of January 2025. Headquarters is the Portland, Oregon office on the third floor of the Foundry Building, and the customer support team works from a small office in Lisbon, Portugal. About half of the company is fully remote, spread across 9 countries.

## Engineering teams

### Infrastructure (SRE)

Marcus Lindqvist is the Head of SRE and leads the Infrastructure team. Infrastructure owns the Kubernetes clusters, Argo CD, the Lattice index cluster, observability tooling, and the on-call program. The team has six engineers, the largest of any engineering team.

### Ingest

Daniel Brandt leads the Ingest team, which owns the Riverbed ingestion pipeline and the Kafka topics it consumes. Jonah Weiss is a Staff Engineer at level E4 on the Ingest team and is the go-to person for Kafka consumer tuning.

### Search Relevance

Aiko Tanaka leads the Search Relevance team, which owns the Prism query service, ranking models, and the LumenBench evaluation suite.

### Identity

Mei Lin is the engineering manager of the Identity team, which owns the Keystone authentication service and customer API key management. Kwame Asante is a Senior Engineer (E3) on Identity.

### API Platform

Sofia Moreau leads the API Platform team, which owns the Beacon API gateway, the public OpenAPI specs, and the SDKs. Sofia also chairs the API Review Board.

### Developer Productivity

Ravi Chandrasekar leads Developer Productivity, which owns CI, the lumenctl internal CLI, and the release tooling. Ravi doubles as the company release manager.

## How to reach people

Slack handles follow the pattern firstname.lastname, for example sofia.moreau. Most leads keep Tuesday and Thursday afternoons free of meetings, so those are good times to ask for a quick call. The most important shared channels are:

- #inc-war-room for active incidents
- #release-train for deploy announcements
- #ask-security for security questions
- #people-ops for HR, benefits, and onboarding questions
- #dev-productivity for CI and tooling problems

## Time zones

Leadership and most engineers work Pacific time. The Lisbon support team works Western European time, which is usually 8 hours ahead of Portland. When scheduling meetings that include Lisbon, aim for 08:00 to 10:00 Pacific so nobody in Lisbon has to stay past 18:00.
