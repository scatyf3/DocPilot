# Expense Policy

Document ID: LL-FIN-02. Owner: Owen Pryce, Finance Controller. Effective July 1, 2024.

This policy explains which business expenses Lumen Labs pays for, how to get approval, and how to get reimbursed. The guiding principle is simple: spend company money the way you would spend your own when it is for a good reason. If something is not covered here, ask in the #finance channel before you spend.

## Submitting expenses

- Submit every expense in Expensify within 30 days of the purchase date.
- An itemized receipt is required for any single expense of $25 or more. A card statement alone is not enough.
- Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll run after your manager approves them.
- Expenses submitted more than 90 days after the purchase date are not reimbursed, with no exceptions.

## Corporate cards

Managers and employees who travel more than four times a year receive a Brex corporate card. Brex transactions still need a receipt and a short business purpose in Expensify within 30 days. Personal purchases on a corporate card are prohibited; if one happens by mistake, repay it within 7 days and tell Finance.

## Approvals

- Your manager approves every expense report.
- Any single purchase over $1,000 needs written pre-approval from your manager before you buy it.
- Any single purchase over $5,000 needs pre-approval from your VP and from the Finance Controller.
- Software subscriptions and other SaaS tools also need a vendor security review before purchase, as described in the Information Security Policy.

## Travel

All business travel is booked through Navan so that Finance can see trips and support you if plans change.

- Flights: book economy for flights shorter than 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer. Business class is not reimbursed.
- Hotels: the nightly cap is $250, or $325 in New York, San Francisco, and London.
- Meals while traveling: a per diem of $75 per day covers all meals, tips, and snacks; you do not need meal receipts when using the per diem.
- Ground transport: rideshare and public transit are fine. Rental cars need a short justification, such as visiting several customer sites in one day.
- Personal car use is reimbursed per mile at the current IRS mileage rate.

## Meals and team events

Each team has a team events budget of $60 per person per quarter for activities like team lunches or an escape room. Unused budget does not roll over to the next quarter. Meals with customers or partners are reimbursed up to $120 per person including tip, and the expense must list the names of the customer attendees. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at team events that your manager has approved in advance.

## Learning and development

Expenses paid from the annual learning and development stipend described in the Benefits and PTO guide must be filed under the expense category L&D. Conference tickets count against the stipend, but travel to an approved conference is paid from your team's travel budget instead of the stipend.

## Not reimbursable

- Traffic fines and parking tickets, even if you were on company business.
- Seat, cabin, or hotel room upgrades.
- Personal entertainment such as in-flight movies or hotel minibars.
- Gym memberships, which are covered by the wellness stipend instead.
- Lost luggage or personal items, unless covered by the travel insurance included with Navan bookings.

## Audits

Finance audits a random 10% sample of expense reports every quarter. If an audit finds an expense that breaks this policy, the employee must repay it within 30 days. Repeated violations are escalated to the employee's VP.
